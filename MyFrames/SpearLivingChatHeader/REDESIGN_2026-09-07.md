## Design decision

The baseline divides attention among large navigation circles, a support stripe, an outlined utility bar, a separate identity chevron, and repeated brand markers. Support status is comparatively faint. The desired outcome is to recognize the conversation, understand its state, and reach its existing actions with less visual competition.

Three structurally different directions were considered:

1. A centered identity placard with utilities below: clear identity, but consumes more transcript height and is sensitive to long names.
2. A leading identity and attached context reading column, with inline utility disclosure: selected for persistent context, fewer visual compartments, direct actions, and a narrow integration seam.
3. A compressed identity capsule with a separate utility inspector: compact at rest, but introduces a new presentation/focus owner and moves established actions away from their existing flow.

The selected direction is expressed in actual package source. It has not received a rendered visual acceptance verdict or a design score.
